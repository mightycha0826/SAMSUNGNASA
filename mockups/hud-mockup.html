How the Point system is applied

      
    
        
  • Two accents only. Cobalt marks what is current: floor arrows, the destination, your position, the active layer. Orange marks the one thing to watch: a warn alert.
    • 
        
  • Transparent status HUD with high-contrast outlines. The status card keeps a transparent background so passthrough surroundings remain unobstructed, relying on crisp white-and-black dual outlines and shadowed typography for legibility in bright or dark rooms.
    • 
        
  • Red is for critical errors. Critical alerts and obstacles use red (and orange for warn perimeter frames), always with an icon and a word, never colour alone (WCAG 1.4.1).
    • 
        
  • High-legibility typography. Atkinson Hyperlegible paired with Pretendard, tabular numerals, and −0.02em tracking on large values to prevent character ambiguity (such as 0/O or 1/I/l).
    • 
        
  • Shape. Cards 20, rows and toasts 16, segmented control 14 with a 10 selected cell, scaled to degrees.
    • 
        
  • No explanatory sentences in the HUD. Titles, data and state words only.
    • 
        
  • Slow pulse. The full-viewport perimeter frame pulses once every 1.6 s in both Warn (orange) and Critical (red) states, well under three flashes per second (WCAG 2.3.1).
    • 
      

    

    

      
Behaviour this mockup settles

      
    
        
  • Status card sits top left with a transparent face. Bottom left collides with the palm map; the top strip keeps route toasts clear below it.
    • 
        
  • Full-viewport alert frame & centre-gaze alerts. The flashing perimeter attaches to the entire viewport edge, while Warn and Critical alert cards render dead-centre in the user’s direct line of sight.
    • 
        
  • Acknowledge alert stops the perimeter pulse in both Warn and Critical states. The frame stays dim and the alert card stays until alert:clear.
    • 
        
  • Several alerts. The card shows the highest level and a “+1” tag for the rest.
    • 
        
  • Critical clears. The route returns to the previous target.
    • 
        
  • Link lost. The last suit temperature stays, greyed, tagged “last value”.
    • 
        
  • Layers are exclusive: one at a time.
    • 
        
  • Units. Air temperature is shown in °C, converted from airTempK.
    • 
        
  • Distance shows distanceM as sent, in room metres.
    • 
        
  • Obstacles are danger red with a hatch and a label.
    •
