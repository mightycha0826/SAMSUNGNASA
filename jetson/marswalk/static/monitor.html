Connecting to the hub Camera lens tilt
Shows what the app draws, from the wearer's head: HUD, map, menus, pointer, arrows, obstacle outline, plus a 1 m floor grid and dots on the hand joints. The room is behind it when the headset's browser gave the app a camera. If the grid does not lie flat on the real floor, set the camera lens at the top right: field of view until the 1 m squares fit the floor near and far, tilt until the grid sits on the floor. The hub keeps the values.
